# Sovrnti

![Sovrnti](logo.png)

Compare sovereign products in AI tools you already use. Every product is scored on Innovation, Control and Scale, for your market and your country.

Make the technology decision. Not just the sovereignty argument.

Vendor claims are not easy to compare. A good choice depends on your specific context. And your evidence still has to survive procurement, finance, security and executive scrutiny.

Sovrnti reviews every option through three lenses: Innovation, Control and Scale. We test products beyond contracts and decks, so that trade-offs are out in the open.

Bring benchmark data into the AI tools you already use. Query any market, geography or snapshot on demand.

Ask questions like "who leads this market", "what holds up if sovereignty is non-negotiable" and "how do these 2 products compare". Look at a market head-on (Snapshot), ranked by raw capability (Capability Index), ranked under full sovereignty rules (Sovrnti Max), or as the gap between the two (Delta). Pin any view to a country, a region or the world view.

Every score rests on public, checkable evidence and, where we can, our own hands-on testing.

A free Sovrnti account to get started. Discovery tools and snapshots are free; deeper views use credits from your account.

## Connect

In Claude, add Sovrnti from the directory. Then connect it on the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in with your Sovrnti account.

In any other MCP client, use the server URL `https://graph.sovrnti.io/mcp`. The [quickstart](https://docs.sovrnti.io/mcp/quickstart) shows each client.

## What this plugin runs and sends

This plugin contains no code. It holds one connector: the Sovrnti MCP server at `https://graph.sovrnti.io/mcp`.

When your AI assistant calls a Sovrnti tool, we receive the tool's name, its inputs and your token. We also see the assistant's software name and the connection's IP address. We do not receive your conversation with the assistant. We keep a record of each call (who, which tool, when, the result) and a one-way hash of the inputs. We do not store the inputs themselves. Our tools never return personal data.
